## Patch Notes 14.0

The 14.0 patch is now live on every platform. Read the complete patch notes down below.
*Features that have changed since the PTS’ Patch Notes are flagged with this icon:* 🟡

## NEW FEATURES

### New PvP map: Sanctum

Sanctum is a medium-sized PvP map built for dynamic and exciting battles. The map features open combat areas, fast routes to key engagement zones and a central area designed as the main hotspot for intense firefights.

### New PvP map: Facility

Facility is one of the most compact PvP maps designed for fast-paced and intense combat. The map maximises opportunities for flanking attacks, rotations and outmaneuvering the enemy.

### New Enemy: Scarab Occult Terminator Sorcerer

Meet the new Terminus enemy: the Scarab Occult Terminator Sorcerer, part of the mighty Thousand Sons. He wields powerful magic for both close and long-range attacks, and is also capable of creating potent illusions.

### New Feature: New Medals and Scores for PvP

Medals and Achievements: enhanced visual effects that highlight a player’s accomplishments during a match. They affect match statistics and the experience points a player earns.

### New Feature: Trials of Resolve #2

Four new challenging Ordeals to get new items have been added, designed for experienced players.

### New Feature: Wargear 2.01

*This is a small intermediate update to the wargear system. *
*We are adding a 3rd Wargear slot to some classes so tabards and capes could be equipped at the same time. *
*This is why some tabards were removed from some chest pieces in this patch in preparation to be moved to a separate slot. *
*With Patch 15 you will be able to equip everything together.*
*In addition to that, with the next update robes and tabards are coming to Vanguard and Heavy.*
*Capes for Bulwark are also in the works, however it will take a bit more time (not in Patch 15), since robes need to be removed from chest pieces first.*
*Oh and "Something we should have done a long time ago" customization feature also coming in Patch 15 :)*
Robes for the Assault and Tactical classes have been added, along with tabards for the Assault class which can be earned by completing new Ordeals.

### New Feature: Weekly Rewards for Participation in PvP

Earn Accolades for participating in PvP on weekly basis

### New Feature: PvP Team Reshuffle

At the end of a PvP match players will be randomly assigned to teams based on match results (experimental feature, will be turned on and off on backend)

### New Heroic Items

- Added 6 Heroic Weapons (Thunder Hammer, Power Fist, Bolt Carbine, Melta Rifle, Power Sword, Stalker Bolt Rifle) to unlock with Accolades.

- Added 4 Heroic Armour (Deathwatch Hand, Deathwatch Chest, Ultramarine Chapter Champion Pauldron) parts to unlock with Accolades.

### New Season Pass Content

- Salamanders Champion with its Signature Multi-Melta Weapon Skin.
- Raptors Cosmetic Pack.

- Damage increased by 15%.

**Omnissian Axe**

### New Heroic Weapons

🟡* Following your feedback on the PTS, the 6 new Heroic weapons received buffs*:
🟡 **Salamanders Power Sword**
*We understand that a lot of you wanted it to be a burning blade, but only Guilliman gets to wield one.*
- Fixed perk activation, now it should work according to description and should affect only light attacks.
- Increased max stacks from 5 to 6.
- Increased number of stacks you gain per perfect parry from 1 to 3.

🟡 **Combi-Flamer Bolt Carbine**
- Increased damage by 20% for bolter barrel.
- Increased burn status effect accumulation speed for flamer barrel.
- Fixed ammo refill for flamer barrel.

🟡 **Deathwatch Stalker Bolt Rifle**
- Decreased number of body shots to activate perk effect from 5 to 4.
- Perk now activates on any body shots, not on body shots on a single target.
- Increased headshot damage multiplier to match Relic versions.
- Increased perk damage bonus to 30% versus minoris, majoris and extremis enemies and to 10% versus terminus enemies.

🟡 **Salamanders Melta Rifle**
- Increased damage fall-off range.
- Increased damage by 10%.

🟡** Deathwatch Power Fist**
- Improved animation responsiveness for entering melta blast stance.
- Fixed tracking for the melta blast move.
- Greatly increased direct damage for melta blast move.
- Melta blast burn effect AoE is slightly wider.
- Added directional input to melta blast move.

🟡 **Lord Executioner's Axe**
- Now properly restores contested health with new attacks
