# Hotfix 15.1

## **GAMEPLAY AND BALANCING TWEAKS**

### **Melee in PvE**

**One Handed Thunder Hammer**

- Base damage is increased by 10%.
- Temporary removed forced stagger from charging stances.

*It will return in the next patch, once we make it less spammable.*

- Underhand Swing damage is increased by 67%.
- Echoing Swing waves damage is increased by 100%.
- Energised Strike damage is increased by 100% and damage radius is increased by 50%.
- Improved controls responsiveness after Energised Strikes.
- Charged attacks damage is increased by 25%. Greatly improved cleave.


**One Handed Thunder Hammer Perks**

- *Charge Overflow*: Duration is increased to 20 seconds.
- *Rapid Recharge*: Cooldown is decreased to 15 seconds.
- *Deflective Charge*: Cooldown is decreased to 20 seconds.
- *Reverberating Slam*: Fixed an issue that the second hit sometimes doesn't deal proper damage and not affected by perks.
- *Perfect Reprisal*: Now affects all Light Attacks for its 7 second duration, not the first one.
- *Preparedness* and* Brutal Reforge*: Are now triggered only by One Handed Hammer kills, rather than while the One Handed Hammer is equipped.


**Heroic One Handed Thunder Hammer - Power Maul**

- Damage bonus for each charge is increased to 30%.



### **Firearms in PvE**

**Melta Rifle**

- *Standard-Issue version and all non range-specialized versions*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 2 to 3 meters.
- Default max ammo pool is increased by 2 (now 12).
- Default magazine size is increased by 1 (now 6).
- Magazine size and max ammo pool is increased by 1 for *Master-Crafted - Beta*,* Drogos Reclamation - Alpha *and *Ophelian Liberation - Alpha *versions.
- *Master-Crafted - Alpha version*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 2.5 to 5 meters.
- *Salvation of Bakka version*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 3 to 5 meters.
- *Gathalamor Crusade version*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 3.5 to 5 meters


**Heavy Melta Rifle**

- *Standard-Issue version and all non range-specialized versions*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 2 to 3 meters.
- *Master-Crafted - Alpha version*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 3 to 6 meters.
- *Salvation of Bakka version*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 4 to 6 meters.
- *Gathalamor Crusade version*: Damage fall off distance start point is changed from 5 to 6 meter.



### **Pickups**

- Fixed an unintended consequence of a previous bug fix concerning relics spawn outside of the playable area which made relic spawns became more deterministic on higher difficulty levels. 
- Fix also affects low spawn rate pickups such as Geneseed, which should not spawn outside of the playable area on levels that have branching paths anymore.




## **CRUCIBLE**

- Fixed a bug where a previously selected Operation and difficulty would affect the Crucible Mode.
- Added a “*Not Enough Penances*” notification if too few penances are selected.
- Fixed a bug where "*Guardian Glyph*" Penance would not revive a player affected by the “Curse of the Warp” Penance.
- Fixed a bug where “*Pandemonium*” Penance did not increase the Majoris enemy type spawn rate in some cases.
- “*Pandemonium*” Penance value is adjusted from 15% to 25%.
- Fixed a bug where “*Madnes*s” Penance did not increase the number of enraged Majoris enemies in some cases.
- Fixed a bug where a player below Level 25 could enter a Crucible mission when two Level 25 players entered the ready zone;
- "Quick Match" window is updated to display “Bonus Crucible XP” and “Crucible XP” separately.
- "*Corruptive Miasma*'' Penance is disabled during certain boss sequences like Heldrake and Hierophant.




## **CHAOS SIEGE MODE**

- Fixed a bug where players could take damage from friendly chaos portals.
- Summonable Hellbrute melee damage has been decreased by 30%.
- Fixed several issues with weapons not using Chaos skins in Chaos Siege.
- Loyalist Marines are much more effective in killing cultists in Chaos Siege.




## **CUSTOMISATION**

- Fixed an issue with hoods not being recolourable.
- Fixed an issue with certain robes not being recolourable.
- Fixed an issue where cloth customization did not save between restarts.
- Lots of minor fixes and improvements.

## **QUALITY OF LIFE**

- Added a small invulnerability window when the player rises up from knockback.

## **GENERAL FIXES**

- Fixed: Auspex Scan buffs Gunstrike damage by default without any perks, behavior restored to patch 14.2.
- Fixed a bug with the Heroic Bolt Rifle’s Melta shot dealing different amounts of damage compared to the primary slot when equipped in the secondary slot using the “Tactical Reserve” perk.
- PvP: Fixed a bug with Abilities for all classes, including the Helbrute, recharge twice as fast as intended in some cases.
- Fixed a bug with the “*Elusive Fire*” perk not working with Heroic Melta-rifle.
- Fixed a bug where some Chaos Space Marine voiceovers were unprocessed in Chaos Siege.
- Campaign: Fixed a bug where players could spawn without the Thunder Hammer in the campaign mission *Dawn's Descent*.
- Lots of minor UI and localization fixes.
- Crash fixes and general stability improvements.
